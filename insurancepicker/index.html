FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need private insurance at all?

No. ÖGK covers contract doctors, emergencies, necessary operations in public hospitals and most prescriptions. Private cover buys comfort, choice and sometimes speed.

How do private doctor bills get paid back?

Send the bill to ÖGK first; it refunds a small part. Then send it to your insurer. Generali pays 100% of the rest if ÖGK paid something, 80% if not. We use 80% to be safe.

What if we move out of Austria?

Generali covers treatment abroad only while Austria is your home (more than 6 months a year). After you move, it covers Austria only, and you can cancel at the end of the month you move. Ask about a standby policy (Anwartschaft) if you might come back. ÖGK ends and you join your new country’s system. Cigna and Allianz Care are built to move with you; the price can change.

What about pregnancy and having a baby?

ÖGK already covers the Mutter-Kind-Pass check-ups and a birth in a public hospital. Private plans only pay if the pregnancy starts after their waiting period: 9 months at Generali. Generali Premium covers a private-room birth (the €300-a-day Vienna excess applies), or pays €1,517 cash if you use the public ward. Basic and Classic pay daily cash for at least 6 days. Wiener Städtische covers midwife costs. PassportCard pays up to €25,000 per policy for pregnancy and birth after a 12-month wait. Cigna and Allianz Care include maternity in the core plan after a 12-month wait; Cigna Platinum pays up to €11,000 per birth and €22,000 for complications.

Do the plans work in Israel and the USA?

Generali’s travel cover pays for emergencies on trips up to 6 weeks anywhere in the world, USA included, up to €250,000. It doesn’t cover planned treatment or living there. PassportCard covers Israel through Clalit (with Clalit’s usual co-pays) but only emergencies in the USA. Cigna and Allianz Care cover Israel in their cheaper “worldwide excluding USA” area. In the USA that area only pays emergencies on trips (Cigna: emergency hospital stays up to 21 days; Allianz Care: emergency treatment on trips up to 6 weeks), unless you pay for the pricier worldwide area. Both are for expats: Cigna doesn’t sell its EU plan to citizens of the country they live in, and covers your country of citizenship for up to 180 days a year. ÖGK only refunds a small amount at Austrian rates outside the EU.

What about existing health conditions?

Declare everything. Treatment that started before the contract is never covered. A condition you declared is covered unless the insurer excludes it in writing.

Does private cover get me surgery faster?

It can help, but no insurer guarantees a date. Ask the surgeon for real dates first.

Will my premium go up?

Probably. Generali’s tariffs rose 1–7% a year on average over the last five years, and the 5% partner discount ends after year three.
